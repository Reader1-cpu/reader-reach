Community Rules

1. Stay connected to the book.
Keep discussion relevant to the selected book and its subject.
2. Respect other readers.
Disagree with ideas without attacking or insulting other people.
3. No harassment.
Do not threaten, target, intimidate, or repeatedly disturb another participant.
4. No spam.
Do not flood the discussion with repeated, irrelevant, or promotional messages.
5. Respect authors.
Questions and criticism are welcome when expressed constructively.
6. Protect private information.
Do not post another person's private contact information or personal details.
7. Follow the session time.
Each scheduled book discussion closes automatically after 30 minutes.
