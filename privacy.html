Privacy Policy

How we respect and handle information shared through the Reader Reach community.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Reader Reach handles personal information when people visit our website, create accounts, discover books, submit author profiles or books, participate in community features, or contact our support team.

2. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide, such as your email address, display name, account details, author biography, profile image, author website, book information, reading-shelf selections, community posts, support messages, and files you choose to submit. We may also process basic technical and sign-in information needed to operate and secure the website.

3. How we use information

We use information to manage accounts and sign-ins, review author and book submissions, display approved profiles and books, support reading shelves, show completed-reader totals, operate The Reading Room, respond to enquiries, deliver relevant service notifications, and protect the platform against misuse.

4. What other people can see

Approved author profiles, book listings, and content posted in public community areas may be visible to other visitors. Reading activity may contribute to book-level totals without displaying every reader’s identity. Please avoid including sensitive personal details in publicly visible fields or posts.

5. Service providers and external links

We use trusted technology and service providers to host, maintain, secure, and operate Reader Reach. They may process information on our behalf only as needed to provide their services, subject to applicable arrangements and safeguards. Reader Reach may link to authors’ websites and other independent services. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit those websites.

6. Cookies and sign-in information

Reader Reach may use cookies or browser storage necessary for sign-in sessions, site operation, and security. If we introduce optional tracking or marketing technologies, we will update this policy and provide any choices required by applicable law.

7. Messages and notifications

We may use your contact information to respond to support enquiries and send account or service messages. Where enabled, readers may receive notifications about new Reading Room topics or other relevant activity. We will provide legally required choices for non-essential communications.

8. Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information and restrict access according to users’ roles and operational needs. However, no website or online service can guarantee complete security.

9. How long information is kept

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant features, address requests, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations. Some information may remain in backups or records for a limited period after an account or item is removed.

10. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, ask for inaccurate details to be corrected, request deletion, object to certain uses, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Younger users

If personal information relating to a child is provided without the permissions or protections required by applicable law, please contact Reader Reach so we can review the situation.

12. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or information practices change. The latest version will be available on this page, and we will provide additional notice where required.

13. Contact Reader Reach

For questions about this policy or requests relating to personal information, please contact the Reader Reach Support Team through our Contact page. Privacy enquiries should be directed to Reader Reach support, not to individual authors.
