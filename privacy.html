Privacy Policy

How Reader Reach handles account, author, reader and community information.

Draft prepared: 24 October 2026 · For review before publication

This page is a draft. Reader Reach should verify its legal identity, contact details, data practices and operational procedures before public launch.

1. Who we are

Reader Reach operates an online literary discovery and community website. For privacy enquiries and requests, contact us through the Reader Reach Contact page. Before public launch, Reader Reach should publish the legal name and business contact details of the person or entity responsible for determining how personal information is used.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Reader Reach, we may process your email address, login and account identifiers, display name, reader or author role, author biography, profile photo, author website link, submitted book information and mockups, reading shelf statuses, completed-book activity, Reading Room posts and replies, support messages and uploaded attachments, notification preferences and technical records needed for security and operation. Some information is public by design, such as approved author profiles, approved book listings and public Reading Room content.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, authenticate users, display books and author profiles, maintain reading shelves and completed-reader counts, moderate submissions, provide support, operate the Reading Room, send service and topic notifications where enabled, prevent abuse and protect the website. Where legally required, we obtain consent; otherwise we rely on an appropriate lawful basis such as providing the requested service, legitimate interests or legal obligations.

4. Public information and your choices

Approved author names, biographies, profile images, book listings and some community contributions may be visible to visitors. Avoid publishing private information in public fields or posts. Shelf activity may contribute to aggregate book reader counts. If you want a public item reviewed or removed, contact Reader Reach.

5. Service providers and international processing

Reader Reach uses GitHub Pages to host website files and Supabase for account authentication, database functions and related infrastructure. Providers may process information in other countries depending on service configuration and their own infrastructure. We should verify the actual project region, subprocessors and safeguards before publishing final cross-border transfer details. Links to third-party book sellers are governed by those third parties’ own privacy notices.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The website and its authentication tools may use cookies or browser storage necessary to maintain sign-in sessions, security and site preferences. Any optional analytics, advertising or non-essential tracking should be disclosed separately and enabled only with the required legal basis or consent. This policy does not claim that tracking tools are absent without an audit.

7. Notifications and emails

If Reader Reach enables email or in-app notifications about new Reading Room topics or other service activity, we may use account contact details and book activity to deliver those notices. We will explain and implement appropriate preferences or unsubscribe controls for non-essential messages before activating them.

8. How long we keep information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, meet legal requirements and protect the platform. The exact retention periods and deletion procedures should be documented before launch. Public posts, backups and security records may have different lawful retention needs.

9. Security

We use technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service, including account authentication and access restrictions. No online system is perfectly secure. Users should protect passwords and report suspected account misuse promptly.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent where consent applies. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority, including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where it has jurisdiction. Contact Reader Reach to make a request; we may need to verify your identity.

11. Children

Reader Reach is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without the permissions and safeguards required by applicable law. If you believe a child’s information has been submitted improperly, please contact us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, service providers or legal obligations change. Material changes will be communicated where appropriate, and the updated date will be shown on this page.

13. Contact

To ask questions or exercise privacy rights, use the Reader Reach Contact page. We will provide any additional legally required business identity and contact information before wider public promotion.
