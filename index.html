About Us

A platform built around books and the people who bring them to readers.

Reader Reach connects readers, authors, moderators and administrators through a structured platform for discovering books, sharing conversations, coordinating features and building meaningful reader communities.

Authors can build approved profiles and submit multiple books. Readers can discover authors and books, follow authors and participate in discussions.
