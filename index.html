About Reader Reach

A place built around books and the people who read them.

Reader Reach is designed to make book discovery more intentional. Authors can present their work with useful context, while readers can explore books, previews, discussions, and features in one connected space.

The platform is built around approved access and real submissions rather than invented public profiles.

Built for real books.

Approved authors, approved books, real schedules, real discussions, and protected Flipper Guides will replace these placeholders as Reader Reach grows.
