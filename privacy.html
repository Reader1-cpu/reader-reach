1. About Reader Reach and our purpose

Reader Reach is a literary discovery and community platform created to help authors find their readers and help readers discover books, short stories, and the people behind them. Our founder is also a writer whose work has reached many readers. Inspired by that experience, she wanted to create a space where other authors could connect with people who may appreciate their stories.

Reader Reach provides author profiles, book discovery, reading shelves, trailers, and community features such as The Reading Room. Reader Reach is not a bookstore, payment service, or publishing contract. Where an author provides an external purchase link, any purchase takes place outside Reader Reach.

A Book Flipper is an interactive digital presentation that allows visitors to explore a book or an approved preview through a page-turning experience. A completed, accessible Book Flipper is required before The Curator can grant final approval for a book to appear publicly on Reader Reach. Authors are responsible for ensuring they have the rights to display any content included in their Flipper.

6. Reading shelves and reader counts

Readers can save books, mark them as currently reading, or mark them as completed. Reader counts reflect activity recorded on Reader Reach and are not proof of book purchases or independently verified reading. Creating fake accounts or manipulating reader counts is prohibited.

7. The Reading Room

Approved authors may start a topic about their own eligible book after it reaches 10 distinct completed readers on Reader Reach. Readers who are currently reading or have completed that book may participate according to the feature's access rules. Discussions may include spoilers. Harassment, threats, spam, impersonation, and sharing copyrighted material without permission are prohibited.
