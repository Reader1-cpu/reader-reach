1. About Reader Reach

Reader Reach is an online literary discovery and community platform. Readers can discover authors, books and short stories, maintain reading shelves and participate in eligible Reading Room activities. Authors can submit their profiles and books for review. Reader Reach does not itself sell books or process payments for books. Links may direct visitors to an author’s website or an independent seller.

By using the platform, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the service. You must be legally able to agree to these Terms, or have any permission required by applicable law. Do not misrepresent your age or identity.

You are responsible for activity under your account except where applicable law provides otherwise. Reader Reach may restrict access to protect users or investigate misuse.

Authors must have the rights and permissions necessary for every submitted element, including third-party artwork, photographs, music and video. Reader Reach does not acquire book publishing or sales rights by approving a submission.

Reader Reach may review, reject, suspend or remove content that violates these Terms, infringes rights, creates safety concerns or is otherwise unsuitable for the community. Users can report concerns through the Contact page.

6. Reading shelves and reader counts

Readers may mark books as saved, currently reading or completed. Completed-reader counts and related milestones reflect activity recorded on Reader Reach, not independently verified book sales or reading. Users must not manipulate counts through false or duplicate accounts.

7. The Reading Room

Approved authors may start topics for their own eligible books after the platform records at least 10 distinct completed readers for that book. Readers who are currently reading or have completed the relevant book may participate according to the feature’s access rules. Topics may contain spoilers. Users must engage respectfully and must not post harassment, threats, unlawful content, spam or unauthorized copyrighted material.

8. Recognition and Spotlight features

Book Spotlight placements, scheduling and Literary Distinction recognition are subject to Reader Reach review and feature rules. A book reaching 50 distinct completed readers may qualify for Curator review; recognition is not automatic and is not a guarantee of commercial success. Feature rules may be updated prospectively and communicated where appropriate.
